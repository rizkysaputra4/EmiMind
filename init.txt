You are a senior AI architect, financial data engineer, quantitative researcher, and fintech product strategist.

I am planning to build and commercialize an AI financial research platform specifically for the Indonesian Stock Exchange (IDX).

IMPORTANT PRODUCT THESIS

The core product is NOT an AI stock screener.

The core product is:

"An AI chat that deeply understands the context and history of Indonesian publicly listed companies."

Existing Indonesian securities platforms already have large amounts of data:
- Financial statements
- Key statistics
- Stock prices
- Historical prices
- Charts
- News
- Corporate actions
- Analyst information
- Company profiles
- Some AI-generated analysis

However, their AI integration is usually limited.

The user may receive an AI-generated analysis, but cannot freely challenge it, ask follow-up questions, investigate the reasoning, compare it against historical information, or continue a deep conversation while maintaining the entire company context.

Generic LLMs such as ChatGPT have excellent reasoning capabilities, but they don't inherently have a comprehensive, structured, longitudinal knowledge base of IDX companies.

A user currently has to manually:
1. Open a securities platform
2. Find financial data
3. Screenshot/copy the data
4. Feed it into an LLM
5. Ask a question
6. Find another report
7. Feed more context
8. Repeat the process

This is inefficient and the context is fragmented.

The product should eliminate this friction.

The desired experience is:

User:
"Why did BMRI's net profit increase even though revenue declined?"

AI:
Provides an evidence-backed explanation.

User:
"But NIM improved. Doesn't that contradict your conclusion?"

AI:
Understands the previous discussion and retrieves the relevant data.

User:
"Was this already happening in Q4 2025?"

AI:
Retrieves historical context.

User:
"Compare this with BBCA and BBRI."

AI:
Retrieves the equivalent data from peers and compares them.

User:
"Is the market already pricing this in?"

AI:
Combines financial fundamentals, valuation, historical price behavior, and recent events.

User:
"Challenge the bear case."

AI:
Builds an evidence-based counterargument.

The key product concept is:

CONTEXT-AWARE FINANCIAL CONVERSATION.

The product should feel like having an AI research analyst who already knows the company's history.

--------------------------------------------------
MEMORY DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------

Design a memory system with at least these layers:

1. RAW DOCUMENT MEMORY

Original PDFs and extracted text.

This is the ultimate source of truth.

2. FINANCIAL FACT MEMORY

Structured numerical facts.

Example:

{
  company: "BMRI",
  metric: "net_profit",
  period: "2026-H1",
  value: ...,
  currency: "IDR",
  source: "...",
  page: ...
}

3. DERIVED FINANCIAL MEMORY

Deterministically calculate:

- YoY
- QoQ
- HoH
- CAGR
- Margins
- Growth acceleration
- Historical averages
- Percentiles
- Record highs/lows
- Guidance deviations
- Peer-relative metrics

Do NOT repeatedly ask the LLM to calculate these.

4. NARRATIVE MEMORY

Management claims, explanations, strategic statements, risks, expectations, and commentary.

5. RELATIONSHIP MEMORY

Examples:

Revenue decline
→ caused by lower segment X contribution

Profit increase
→ despite revenue decline
→ because credit cost declined

Management guidance
→ actual result
→ exceeded guidance

Corporate event
→ stock price reaction

6. LONG-TERM ISSUER MEMORY

Higher-level context such as:

- Historical business characteristics
- Recurring themes
- Management strategy
- Long-term risks
- Historical patterns
- Management credibility
- Repeated explanations
